Fish suggestions, completion, shell history and prompt styling are shell-owned. No credit is taken for reproducing them.

Inspection findings: parser has a byte stream and saved partial-parse state; VT100Terminal executes tokens into VT100Screen. PTYTask delegates process/server lifecycle to job managers and reads in bounded batches with documented backpressure. PTYSession joins task, screen and views. The Metal driver has separate background/text/cursor/image renderers. Profiles use NSUserDefaults; DVR encodes screen states, not just raw output. Aster rewrites these boundaries and only directly adapts the identified OSC algorithms at this stage.
